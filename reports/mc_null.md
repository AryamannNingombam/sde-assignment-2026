# Monte Carlo of the experiment decision rule (C6)

Generated by `python scripts/mc_null.py`; applies `controller.exp_rules.daily_look` and `controller.exp_alloc` unchanged.

Setup: 3000 dials/day at pickup 0.3 (about 900 connects/day), engagement 0.5, 8 days, 4500 leads with intra-lead correlation 0.1, one control and two challengers entering at 7.5% (ramp to 20% after >= 1 day and >= 100 connects when the point estimate is >= control, drop at z <= -1), one workflow test (alpha 0.020000000000000004) and two pre-registered half-traffic analysis groups (alpha 0.0025000000000000005 each; 0.025 per dim, 0.05 per workflow over its two dims). Decision: <= 4 Lan-DeMets O'Brien-Fleming looks at information fractions 0.25/0.5/0.75/1, Holm across the 2 arms, point lift >= 3%, recent-24h lower bound > 0, pickup non-inferiority (margin 0.02). Looks happen at the end of each day.

| scenario | runs | any promotion | promoted A | promoted B | median day of A's promotion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| null (both arms 0%) | 1000 | 1.4% | 0.8% | 0.6% | 4 |
| 10% lift on A, B null | 400 | 34.0% | 33.8% | 0.2% | 4 |
| 15% lift on A, B null | 400 | 62.7% | 62.5% | 0.2% | 4 |

False-winner rate (null, any arm, any scope): **1.4%** (target <= 5%). A null scenario has 4 experiments (2 workflows x 2 dims), so the chance of at least one false promotion is about 4x this. Power is the share of runs promoting the truly better arm A within the 8-day run.
